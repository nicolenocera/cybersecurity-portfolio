This room was my first real hands-on experience with exploiting a vulnerable machine from start to finish. It combined everything I’ve been learning from enumeration to exploitation into one full walkthrough.

I started by scanning the target with Nmap to find open ports and services. Then I used what I found to dig deeper, eventually discovering a file upload vulnerability that let me gain access to the system.

After getting in, I practiced privilege escalation by looking at system info, checking running processes, and finding ways to elevate to root. It showed me how attackers move through a system once they’re inside.

This room helped me understand how important good enumeration is. Missing one open port or detail can throw off the whole process. It also gave me a huge confidence boost since I was able to complete the full chain on my own.

This room gave me an overview of what the blue team actually does in cybersecurity. It covered the main responsibilities of defenders — like detecting intrusions, monitoring logs, and responding to threats in real-time.

I learned about common tools and systems used in defensive roles, like SIEMs (Security Information and Event Management), IDS/IPS systems, and log management. The room also introduced terms like SOC (Security Operations Center), which is where a lot of defenders work from.

One part I liked was learning how attackers leave behind artifacts in logs — and how blue teamers use those to investigate and respond. It gave me a better appreciation of how detailed log analysis can expose suspicious activity.

This room helped me start thinking more like a defender. It showed me how blue team roles rely on both technical knowledge and a good eye for patterns, which is something I’ll be building as I go deeper into security.

## 🔍 Concepts to Remember

- **SIEM (Security Information & Event Management):** Centralizes and analyzes logs from across a network
- **Cyber Kill Chain:** Recon → Weaponization → Delivery → Exploitation → Installation → C2 → Actions
- **IOC (Indicators of Compromise):** Evidence of malicious activity (e.g., IPs, hashes, domain names)
